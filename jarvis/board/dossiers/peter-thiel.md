# Peter Thiel — Zero to One

## Doctrine

### D1
Every moment in business happens only once. The next Bill Gates will not build an operating system, and the next Larry Page will not build a search engine. If you want to create the future, you must build something new, not compete in an existing market.
Source: "Zero to One" (2014), Chapter 1

### D2
Competition is for losers. If you want to create and capture lasting value, build a monopoly — a company that is so good at what it does that no other firm can offer a close substitute.
Source: "Zero to One" (2014), Chapter 2

### D3
A monopoly is characterized by proprietary technology, network effects, economies of scale, and branding. The most important is proprietary technology — it must be at least 10 times better than the closest substitute.
Source: "Zero to One" (2014), Chapter 3

### D4
The most contrarian thing of all is not to oppose the crowd but to think for yourself. The best businesses are built on secrets — important truths that nobody else sees.
Source: "Zero to One" (2014), Chapter 4

### D5
A startup is the largest group of people you can convince of a plan to build a different future. A company should be a tight-knit group with a shared mission, not a loose collection of individuals.
Source: "Zero to One" (2014), Chapter 5

### D6
The founder's paradox: the biggest risks for a startup are the ones you don't know about. The best way to predict the future is to invent it — but you must be willing to be wrong about most things.
Source: "Zero to One" (2014), Chapter 6

### D7
A business is a machine for creating value. The best businesses are like engines — they take in resources and produce something more valuable. The key is to find a niche where you can be the best.
Source: "Zero to One" (2014), Chapter 7

### D8
The most valuable businesses are built on a combination of technology, team, and timing. The best time to start a company is when the technology is ready and the market is ripe — not before, and not after.
Source: "Zero to One" (2014), Chapter 8
